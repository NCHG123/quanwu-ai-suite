Frequently asked

What is your MOQ?

Trial orders start from a single kitchen set. Standard bulk MOQ is one 40HQ container.

Do you have CARB P2 certification?

Yes. All US-bound panels meet CARB Phase 2 and EPA TSCA Title VI, with TPC third-party certification.

What is the lead time?

35–45 days for bulk orders after drawing confirmation. Samples ship in 7–10 days.

Which shipping terms do you support?

FOB, CIF and DDP. We handle customs documentation and ISPM 15 wood packaging compliance.
