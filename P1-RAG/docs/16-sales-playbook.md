# 16. Sales Playbook — Objection Handling & Case Studies (B2B)

> **AUDIENCE LEVEL:** mixed. Scripts in sections 1–3 are PUBLIC (safe for external sales agent). Section 4 (case details) is PUBLIC in anonymized form. Internal margin data stays in doc 15.

## 1. Objection: "Your price is higher than Vietnam / Malaysia suppliers."

**Script:**
"Fair point — unit price alone can look different. Three things to compare before deciding:
1. **Spec-for-spec**: our base offer is CARB P2 plywood box + DTC soft-close hardware. Many lower quotes are MDF box with basic hinges. Compare the spec sheet, not the number.
2. **Landed cost**: we ship flat-pack, which loads roughly twice the cabinet volume per container versus assembled shipping. Lower per-container cost means your landed cost per cabinet often comes out even or better.
3. **Rework cost**: our defect claim rate over the last 3 years is under 0.5%. One reworked installation in the US costs more than the unit saving.
If you share the competing quote's spec sheet, I'll do a line-by-line comparison for you."

## 2. Objection: "How do I know your quality is good? I can't visit the factory."

**Script:**
"Standard ways our partners verify without visiting:
1. **Sample first**: we ship a sample cabinet box in your chosen door finish before any bulk order — cost credited back on your first order.
2. **Live video audit**: our sales manager walks the production line on a video call, including the CNC cutting and edge-banding stations.
3. **Third-party inspection**: SGS / Bureau Veritas pre-shipment inspection is welcome, arranged at buyer's cost.
4. **Certifications**: CARB P2, EPA TSCA Title VI, FSC chain-of-custody — all documented and verifiable with the certificate numbers we provide.
We currently ship to 20+ countries; references in your market can be arranged once we sign an NDA."

## 3. Objection: "Your MOQ is too high for my first order."

**Script:**
"Every partner starts somewhere. Options for a first order:
1. **Trial order**: one full container (40HQ, mixed kitchen + wardrobe) is our standard MOQ — roughly a 20-unit apartment project.
2. **LCL pilot**: we accept LCL (less-than-container) pilot orders from $8,000 FOB, so you can test market response with a small apartment fit-out first.
3. **Sample box**: start with one custom sample cabinet to validate quality and finish.
Most of our US and Middle East distributors started with a single trial container and scaled to quarterly orders within the first year."

## 4. Case Studies (anonymized — safe for external use)

**Case 1 — US multi-family developer (Texas)**
- Need: 240-unit apartment renovation, kitchen + vanity package, strict TSCA compliance.
- Solution: frameless plywood cabinets, PET door finish, flat-pack containers (12 × 40HQ), staged shipments matching their construction phases.
- Result: delivered across 8 months with zero compliance issues; repeat order for phase 2 signed.

**Case 2 — Middle East fit-out contractor (UAE)**
- Need: villa project, 40 kitchens, high-humidity coastal environment, Arabic-style door profiles.
- Solution: marine-grade board spec, lacquer finish on custom profiles, mixed 40HQ + LCL shipments.
- Result: project completed on schedule; contractor added us to their approved vendor list.

**Case 3 — European kitchen studio (Germany)**
- Need: OEM production under their own brand, E1 boards, precise metric dimensions.
- Solution: full OEM with neutral packaging and their logo, FSC-certified boards, 3 rounds of sample approval before production.
- Result: 3 consecutive years of repeat orders; annual volume now above $600,000.

## 5. Talking Points (our USP, in order)

1. **Full-house capability**: kitchen + wardrobe + vanity + solid wood custom from one factory — one supplier, one container, one point of accountability.
2. **Export-grade compliance**: CARB P2 / TSCA / FSC / E1 — paperwork ready for US and EU customs.
3. **Flat-pack engineering**: ~2× container loading efficiency, lower landed cost per cabinet.
4. **OEM/ODM depth**: own tooling and design team; your brand, your profiles, your packaging.
5. **Reliable lead time**: 30–45 days after deposit and drawing approval, with weekly production photo updates.

## 6. Follow-Up Cadence Rules

- Day 1 after quote: confirm receipt, ask for drawings to refine.
- Day 3: share a relevant case study or container-loading plan.
- Day 7: offer sample cabinet.
- Day 14: manager follow-up call/video.
- Quote expiry (day 30): notify that prices require re-confirmation.
- All interactions logged to CRM with next-action date. No lead goes 14 days without a touch.
