# Overseas Cooperation FAQ

Frequently asked questions from overseas B2B clients — renovation companies, developers and cabinet businesses. Answers below reflect our standard policies.

## Small Orders & Trial Orders

Q: Can I place a small trial order before committing to bulk?
A: Yes. We support low-MOQ trial orders — even a single kitchen set — so new partners can verify quality, packing and service. Trial orders pay sample-tier pricing; the cost difference is credited toward your first bulk order.

Q: Do you accept mixed containers with different products?
A: Yes. Kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, vanities, TV units and matching items (countertops, mirrors, interior doors) can share one container. Mixed loading is planned with density-stratified logic so heavy goods never sit on cabinet cartons.

Q: Can you produce under my own brand?
A: Yes. OEM/private-label is standard: your logo on products, cartons and installation manuals. Brand packaging design support included.

## Shipping & Packaging

Q: Flat-pack or fully assembled — which should I choose?
A: Flat-pack (RTA) cuts shipping volume by 30–40% and roughly doubles units per container — best for distributors and programs with site assembly crews. Fully assembled ships faster to install but wastes container volume. Decide before quoting; it changes packing, QC and landed cost.

Q: How do you protect cabinets from moisture during sea freight?
A: Double-wall cartons, PE foam between door faces, corner protectors and moisture barrier film. Board moisture content is controlled at 8–12% before packing. Marine insurance is recommended; loading photos and videos are provided for every shipment.

Q: What is the difference between FOB and CIF?
A: Under FOB we deliver to the Chinese port and load the vessel; your forwarder handles freight. Under CIF we cover ocean freight to your destination port, but you still pay destination charges and customs. DDP (door-to-door, duties paid) is available for selected markets.

Q: How long does the whole process take?
A: Production 30–45 days after drawing and sample sign-off (lacquer/veneer adds 10–15 days), plus 3–7 days finishing and 12–32 days sea transit depending on destination. Plan 5–8 weeks total from order confirmation to site.

## Installation & Support

Q: Do you provide installation support overseas?
A: Every shipment includes installation manuals with exploded diagrams, labeled panels by room/position, and assembly videos for RTA programs. We offer scheduled remote video guidance for your installation teams, train-the-trainer sessions for dealers, and on-site engineer dispatch for premium projects (travel costs borne by the customer).

Q: What happens if parts are damaged in transit?
A: Document with photos on delivery. Confirmed transit damage is replaced free of charge — hardware by express, panels with the next production run. Marine insurance claims are handled with our loading documentation.

Q: Do you include spare parts?
A: Yes — a hardware spare pack (hinges, slides, handles, cams) is included per container, and retained sample records let us produce matching replacement panels years later.

## Compliance & Documents

Q: What documents do you provide for customs clearance?
A: Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, plus ISPM 15 compliance for wood packaging. For US-bound goods: CARB P2 / EPA TSCA Title VI compliance documentation. FSC and CE certificates where specified.

Q: Can you meet US formaldehyde requirements?
A: Yes. Our US-market programs use CARB Phase 2 / TSCA Title VI compliant boards with third-party certification (TPC) — plywood 0.05 ppm, particleboard 0.09 ppm, MDF 0.11 ppm.

## Payment & Samples

Q: What are your payment terms?
A: Standard T/T 30% deposit, 70% balance against copy of shipping documents. Irrevocable L/C at sight accepted for container orders. USD preferred.

Q: Can we get samples first?
A: Yes — door/material samples at material cost, 5–15 days lead time, cost credited against the first bulk order. 1:1 mockup cabinets available for project sign-off.
