# Product Categories & Standard Dimensions

## Product Lines
1. Kitchen cabinets — base cabinets, wall cabinets, tall/pantry cabinets, island units, countertop coordination
2. Wardrobes — sliding door, hinged door, walk-in closet systems, open wardrobe modules
3. Bathroom vanities — wall-hung and floor-standing, moisture-resistant construction
4. Living room — TV units, sideboards, display cabinets, wine cabinets
5. Whole-house packages — door-wall-cabinet integrated systems for apartments and villas
6. Balcony / laundry / storage cabinets — matching finishes across rooms

## Standard Dimensions (US-convention RTA programs)
| Cabinet type | Depth | Height | Width range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base cabinet | 24 in / 610 mm | 34.5 in / 876 mm (36 in with countertop) | 9–48 in, 3 in increments |
| Wall cabinet | 12 in / 305 mm | 12–42 in | 9–48 in, 3 in increments |
| Tall / pantry | 24 in / 610 mm | 84 or 96 in | 12–36 in |
| Toe kick | 3.5 in recess | 3.5 in | full width |

The 18-inch clearance between a 34.5-inch base cabinet top and a 30-inch wall cabinet bottom is the ergonomic standard for appliance placement.

## European / Metric Convention
- Base units: 720 mm high carcass + 150 mm plinth = 870 mm (900 mm with countertop)
- Wall units: 720 mm standard, depths 300–350 mm
- Carcass thickness 16–18 mm; backs 3–9 mm or full panel on request

## Frameless vs Framed Construction
We produce frameless (full-overlay, European style) construction as standard — maximum storage volume, full-overlay doors, 2 mm reveal between doors. Face-framed construction available for North American inset programs on request.

## Modular vs Full Custom
- Modular RTA programs: standardized carcass sizes, fast production, container-efficient, for developers and distributors
- Full custom (made-to-drawing): any dimension to fit the site, made-to-order production, for fit-out contractors and villas

## Matching Items
Countertops, splashbacks, aluminum profiles, LED lighting, mirrors and cabinet-matched interior doors can be consolidated in the same container to give developers a one-stop fit-out package.
