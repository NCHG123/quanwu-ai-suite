# Board Materials Guide

Choosing the right board (carcass material) is the first specification decision for any cabinet program. Standard export carcass thickness is 16–18 mm with edge-banded panels.

## Available Board Types

### 1. Plywood
Cross-laminated wood veneers. Highest screw-holding strength (2–3 times higher pull-out load than particleboard), excellent moisture resistance, warp-resistant. Recommended for kitchen and bathroom cabinets where moisture resilience matters, and for heavy-duty base cabinets, drawer boxes and shelves under sinks. Higher cost.

### 2. MDF (Medium-Density Fibreboard)
Uniform wood fibers, perfectly smooth surface — the best substrate for painted/lacquer finishes, CNC-machined profiled doors (Shaker, raised panel) and thermofoil wrapping. Vulnerable to water unless sealed and edge-banded; lower screw-holding than plywood. Standard choice for painted door programs.

### 3. Particleboard (Chipboard)
Compressed wood particles with melamine face, lowest cost, flat and stable in dry interiors. The volume default for budget wardrobes, TV units and dry-area carcasses. Screw-holding and moisture resistance are the weakest of the three — we recommend moisture-resistant (MR) particleboard for kitchens.

### 4. OSB (Oriented Strand Board)
Structural-grade board used mainly in construction; occasionally used for industrial-style furniture. Complies with EN 300 for structural use.

### 5. Solid Wood
Oak, maple, birch, beech, rubberwood and bamboo available for doors, frames and accents. Solid wood requires moisture control in transit and acclimatization at destination; typically combined with engineered panels for cabinet boxes.

## Common Construction Practice
Most export programs mix materials: plywood carcass + MDF painted doors; or melamine particleboard carcass + melamine/MDF doors. Drawers and under-sink cabinets benefit from plywood regardless of carcass choice.

## Formaldehyde Emission Grades
- CARB Phase 2 / EPA TSCA Title VI (USA, mandatory): hardwood plywood 0.05 ppm, particleboard 0.09 ppm, MDF 0.11 ppm. Third-party certification (TPC) required.
- E1 (EU and most markets, legal baseline): ≤ 0.124 mg/m³ (≈0.1 ppm) per EN 717-1 chamber method.
- E0 (industry term, voluntary): typically < 0.05 ppm — for premium and health-focused markets.
- ENF (Chinese national standard, strictest limit) and NAF (no added formaldehyde) boards available on request.
- FSC Chain-of-Custody certified boards available for all board types; required by many EU/US buyers and public procurement.

## Recommendation by Market
- USA / Canada: CARB P2 / TSCA Title VI compliant plywood or MDF programs — documentation provided per shipment.
- EU / UK / Australia: E1 or E0 boards; FSC strongly recommended for retail and developer procurement.
- Middle East / Southeast Asia / Africa: E1 boards standard; moisture-resistant (MR) grade recommended for coastal humidity.
