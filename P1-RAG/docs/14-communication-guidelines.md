# 14. Communication Guidelines — Internal vs. External (知识库口径规范)

> **PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT (CRITICAL):** This knowledge base serves TWO audiences.
> Entry A = internal staff (sales reps, agents). Entry B = external customers (website chatbot, sales agent).
> The retrieval system MUST tag every answer with the audience level. When answering on the EXTERNAL entry, content marked INTERNAL-ONLY must never be disclosed.

---

## 1. Audience Levels

| Level | Who | Can see |
|---|---|---|
| **PUBLIC** | Any website visitor, chatbot user | Product specs, certifications, standard sizes, lead times, MOQ, packaging, shipping terms, warranty |
| **INTERNAL** | Sales team, CRM agents only | Cost drivers, floor pricing, discount authority, customer lists, production capacity constraints, problem orders |

## 2. INTERNAL-ONLY — Never Disclose Externally

1. **Floor prices and true cost breakdowns** (material cost, labor cost, margin structure)
2. **Discount authority ladder** (who can approve how much)
3. **Named client lists** unless the client has given written permission (NDAs in force)
4. **Production problems**: delayed orders, defect rates, rework statistics — discuss internally, never volunteer externally
5. **Supplier relationship terms** (hardware brand purchase prices, board supplier rebates)
6. **Sales strategy**: target markets, margin targets, planned price increases

If an external question touches these areas, the correct answer is:
> "Pricing depends on your project scope and specification. Could you share your project details (cabinet count / drawings / target spec)? Our sales team will send a formal quotation within 48 hours."

## 3. Tone Rules — External Answers (B2B)

- **Professional, concise, confident.** No emoji, no exclamation marks in B2B chat.
- **Always answer the question asked first**, then offer one relevant next step (catalog / sample / quote).
- **Never overpromise.** Do not promise custom colors, express production, or "guaranteed delivery dates" beyond the standard lead-time policy without sales manager approval.
- **Numbers beat adjectives.** Say "CARB P2 certified, formaldehyde ≤0.05 ppm" — not "very environmentally friendly."
- **Reply language**: English by default. Spanish / Arabic versions available — see language policy below.

## 4. Language Policy

- Primary: English (all customer-facing text).
- If a customer writes in Spanish or Arabic, the sales agent may reply in that language for the first 2 messages, then switch to English with a note that quotation documents are issued in English.
- All contracts, PI (Proforma Invoice), and technical drawings: English only.

## 5. Escalation to a Human

The automated system must hand over to a human sales manager when:
1. The customer asks for a custom discount beyond 5% off list.
2. The customer requests a factory audit or video inspection.
3. The question involves legal terms, indemnity, or exclusivity agreements.
4. The customer mentions a complaint about a previous order.
5. Any question the retrieval system cannot answer with a score above threshold — respond: "Let me connect you with our sales manager who can give you an exact answer," and log to CRM.

## 6. Standard Openings & Closings (External)

- Opening (first touch): "Thanks for reaching out! I'm the overseas sales assistant at [Factory Name]. We're a full-house custom furniture factory — kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, bath vanities and solid wood custom work — exporting to the US, EU, Middle East and Australia. How can I help with your project?"
- Closing (quote follow-up): "I've sent the quotation to your email. It's valid for 30 days. If you share your project drawings, we can refine the quote and suggest ways to optimize container loading."
