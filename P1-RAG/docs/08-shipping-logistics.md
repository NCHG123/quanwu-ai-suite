# Shipping, Packaging & Logistics

## Flat-Pack (RTA) vs Pre-Assembled
This is the biggest landed-cost decision after the specification itself.

### Flat-Pack / RTA
- Panels disassembled and stacked flat in cartons
- Reduces shipping volume by 30–40% vs assembled; flat-pack roughly doubles the number of kitchens per container
- Best for: distributors, RTA retailers, multifamily programs with site assembly crews
- Trade-off: destination labor cost for assembly (significant in high-wage markets)

### Pre-Assembled
- Faster installation on site, consistent factory-built quality
- Ships "air": a built carcass is mostly empty volume — fewer units per container, higher per-unit freight
- Best for: hotel projects, tight installation schedules, premium custom installations

Rule of thumb: decide the packing mode before quoting — it changes packing spec, QC checkpoints, container math and per-unit landed cost.

## Container Selection
| Container | Internal volume | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 20GP | ~28 cbm | Smaller orders, heavier materials |
| 40GP | ~58 cbm | Standard kitchen cabinet shipments |
| 40HQ | ~68 cbm | Large-volume flat-pack programs |

A 40HQ of flat-pack cabinetry typically holds 800–900 carton items; cabinets cube out before they weigh out. Mixed loads (cabinets + tiles + countertops) use density-stratified loading: heavy rigid goods floor-secured with timber blocking and dunnage bags, cabinetry on upper tiers lashed to tie-down points — never heavy freight stacked on cabinet crates.

## Export Packaging Spec
- Double-wall corrugated cartons, 5-ply
- PE foam sheets between finished door faces; corner protectors; moisture barrier film
- Palletizing or hand-load stacking by panel type for maximum density
- Carton marks: product code, quantity, destination project / floor / unit number
- Optional: plywood crate for marble/stone countertops and high-value lacquer doors

## Transit Times (port to port)
- Australia (Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane): 12–18 days
- USA West Coast (LA/Long Beach): 14–20 days
- Canada (Vancouver): 16–22 days
- Europe (Rotterdam/Hamburg): 25–32 days
- Middle East (Dubai/Jeddah): 15–22 days
- Africa (Mombasa/Durban): 20–30 days
Add 3–7 days production finishing, 1–3 days port loading, 2–5 days customs clearance. Total plan: 5–8 weeks from order confirmation to project site.

## Incoterms We Support
- EXW: ex-works, buyer arranges everything
- FOB (most common): we deliver to Chinese port, loaded on vessel; buyer's forwarder takes over
- CIF: we cover freight to destination port; buyer handles destination charges and customs
- DDP: door-to-door with duties paid — available for selected markets

## Customs Documents Provided
Commercial invoice (accurate declared value — under-declaring is illegal and risks confiscation), packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary/ISPM 15 compliance for wood packaging. Container loading photos and videos provided for every shipment.

## Common Pitfalls We Help Avoid
- Booking freight in August–October peak season without surcharge planning
- Skipping marine insurance — finish damage from moisture is the top claim; insurance is cheap relative to replacement
- Choosing LCL for orders that nearly fill a container — per-unit cost balloons
- Misunderstanding CIF: buyer still pays destination charges and customs
