# Design-to-Production Process

## Standard Workflow (custom / project orders)

### Step 1 — Inquiry & Requirements
Customer submits: project type, quantity, floor plans / site measurements (if available), target style and budget range. The clearer the drawings, the faster the quote.

### Step 2 — Design & 3D Proposal
Our designers produce preliminary layouts, shop drawings and 3D renderings. Revisions are discussed and confirmed. Design fee is typically waived once the order is placed. Deliverables: layout drawings with water/electric points marked, 3D renderings, material/color schedule.

### Step 3 — Quotation & Contract
Quotation by material specification, hardware brand and packing mode (flat-pack vs assembled). Trade terms: FOB, CIF, EXW or DDP by destination. Contract confirms specifications, lead time, payment terms.

### Step 4 — Deposit & Production Scheduling
Production starts after deposit receipt (typically 30%). Orders enter the ERP system; a production schedule with milestones is shared.

### Step 5 — Sample / Mockup Gate (recommended for projects)
For hotel and multi-unit projects, a 1:1 physical mockup or door sample is produced first. The customer signs off on color, profile and construction before bulk production — this prevents mass-scale rework.

### Step 6 — Production
Digital flow: cutting list → panel saw → edge banding (PUR available) → multi-row drilling → sorting by barcode → trial assembly. Moisture content controlled at 8–12%.

### Step 7 — Inspection & Packing
100% trial assembly check, then export packing: double-wall cartons, PE foam sheets, corner protectors, moisture barriers. Cartons labeled by building / floor / unit number for project sites.

### Step 8 — Container Loading & Shipment
Loading photos and videos provided per shipment. Full document set: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin.

### Step 9 — Installation Support & After-sales
Installation manuals, assembly videos, labeled panel maps. Remote video guidance for installation teams; spare hardware parts included per container.

## Lead Times (production, after drawing & sample sign-off)
- Semi-custom melamine / thermofoil programs: 30–45 days
- Custom lacquer / veneer finishes: +10–15 days
- Sample orders: 5–15 days
- RTA stock programs: faster, based on stock availability
- Peak season (August–October): add 5–10 days; book shipping early for space

## Measurement Service
For villa and premium projects we support two-round measurement: preliminary measurement for design, then re-measurement after tiles, flooring and ceiling are complete — the second measurement drives final cutting lists.
