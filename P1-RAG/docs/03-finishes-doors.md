# Door Finishes & Styles Guide

The door front defines the look and the price tier of a cabinet program. Each finish has different tooling time, QC considerations and best-use scenarios.

## Available Finishes

### 1. Melamine (Laminate)
The volume default. Wear-resistant, anti-scratch, easy to clean, no painting needed. Available in solid colors and wood grains, matte to gloss surfaces. Lowest cost, shortest lead time, consistent color. Best for: apartment programs, rental properties, budget-conscious distributors.

### 2. Lacquer / Spray Paint (on MDF)
Premium smooth finish, unlimited custom colors (RAL / Pantone matching), available in matte, satin and high-gloss. Requires controlled paint workshop conditions; longer production time. Best for: villa projects, premium kitchens, brand-forward retailers.

### 3. PET / High-Gloss Acrylic
PET (polyethylene terephthalate) film faces: high gloss or super-matte, fingerprint-resistant, UV-stable, no yellowing. Thinner profile than acrylic at lower cost. Popular in modern minimalist programs across Europe and Australia.

### 4. PVC Thermofoil / Vinyl Wrap
MDF door wrapped with PVC film — enables routed profiles (Shaker, raised panel, country styles) in colors without painting. Cost-effective alternative to lacquer for profiled doors. Avoid prolonged high-heat exposure near ovens without heat shields.

### 5. Wood Veneer
Natural wood veneer on MDF or plywood — real wood grain with engineered stability. Stained and lacquered to spec. Premium tier; add 10–15 days production time.

### 6. UV Painted / UV Board
Factory-cured UV finish — hard surface, fast production, mid-price tier between melamine and lacquer.

## Door Styles
- Flat / slab (modern minimalist)
- Shaker (5-piece look, routed MDF or true 5-piece)
- Raised panel (traditional, Victorian, farmhouse)
- Glass-frame doors (aluminum or wood frames, clear / frosted / fluted glass)
- Fluted / slatted (trending in 2024–2026 hospitality and residential)

## Handles & Pulls
Finger-pull (integrated aluminum profile), aluminum strip handles, knob and bar handles in matte black, brushed brass, chrome and champagne finishes. Handle-less (J-pull / Gola profile) systems available for minimalist kitchens.

## Selection Guidance by Project Type
- Multifamily / rental programs: melamine slab or thermofoil Shaker — durability and cost first.
- Boutique hotel / villa: lacquer, veneer or PET with premium hardware.
- Distributor stock programs: melamine and PET — stable SKUs, fast replenishment.
