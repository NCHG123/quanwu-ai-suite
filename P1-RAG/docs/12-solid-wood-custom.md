# Solid Wood Whole-House Custom (整木定制) Product Line

Beyond panel-based full-house customization, we operate a solid wood workshop for premium whole-house woodwork — the segment developers and villa contractors specify for luxury programs.

## Product Scope
1. Solid wood stairs — straight, L-shape, U-shape, curved (spiral) stairs; stringer, cantilever (floating) and closed-riser constructions; treads in oak, beech, teak-look engineered wood
2. Interior wood doors — solid core engineered doors, flush and paneled styles; veneer or lacquer finish; matching frames and architraves
3. Wall panels / boiserie — full-height decorative wood paneling, fluted and slatted panels, wainscot systems
4. Wine cellars & cabinets — climate-tolerant construction, glass display doors, LED lighting
5. Bookcases, study rooms & feature walls — library walls with integrated ladders and lighting
6. Ceiling & beamwork — coffered ceilings, decorative beams for villa living spaces
7. Wardrobe walk-ins (premium tier) — solid wood frames with panel inserts

## Materials
- Species: oak (red/white), ash, beech, walnut, teak (engineered), maple, rubberwood, bamboo
- Construction: solid wood frames with engineered panels (veneer-core plywood) to resist warping across climates — 100% solid wood panels are avoided for export shipments due to humidity differential between production and destination climates
- Finishes: open-pore stain, closed-pore lacquer, natural oil; custom color matching (RAL/Pantone)

## Moisture & Climate Control (critical for export)
Wood is acclimated and finished at 8–12% moisture content. For destinations with extreme humidity differential (e.g., Middle East dryness, Southeast Asia humidity), we adjust construction: engineered cores, sealed back faces, climate-appropriate hardware. This is the number-one cause of solid wood complaints in international trade and is engineered out at specification stage.

## Lead Times
- Stairs and doors: 45–60 days (engineering-heavy)
- Wall panels: 35–50 days
- Combined with panel furniture in one container: yes, with mixed loading plan

## Typical Client Use
- Villa developers: whole-house woodwork packages (doors + stairs + wall panels + wardrobes, unified finish)
- Hotel projects: lobby feature walls, restaurant joinery
- Renovation contractors: luxury residential upgrades

Solid wood line prices at 2–4× panel-furniture equivalents, quoted per drawing. Engineering drawing fee may apply for stairs and is credited at order confirmation.
