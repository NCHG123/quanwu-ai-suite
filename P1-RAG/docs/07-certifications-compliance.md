# Certifications & Market Compliance

Compliance documentation is provided per shipment, not just in brochures. Serious export factories treat CARB P2 / TSCA Title VI and E1 documentation as table stakes.

## Formaldehyde Standards
| Standard | Region | Material scope | Limit | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CARB Phase 2 | USA (California origin, de facto national) | Hardwood plywood | 0.05 ppm | Third-party certification (TPC) |
| CARB Phase 2 | USA | Particleboard | 0.09 ppm | TPC |
| CARB Phase 2 | USA | MDF | 0.11 ppm | TPC |
| EPA TSCA Title VI | Entire USA (federal) | All composite wood | Same as CARB P2 | Mandatory labeling |
| E1 | EU / most markets | Wood-based panels | ≤ 0.124 mg/m³ (EN 717-1) | Legal baseline |
| E0 | Voluntary / premium | Wood-based panels | typically < 0.05 ppm | Industry term |
| ENF | China (strictest national limit) | Wood-based panels | ≤ 0.025 mg/m³ | On request |

US retailers and large buyers (Home Depot, Walmart, Target-type procurement) require CARB P2 / TSCA Title VI compliance in vendor requirements — certificates and test reports must be valid and traceable, with TPC certifier numbers on labels.

## FSC (Forest Stewardship Council)
FSC Chain-of-Custody (CoC) certification tracks wood from responsibly managed forests through the supply chain. Increasingly required by EU/US retailers, corporate procurement and government projects. FSC-certified boards typically command a 10–20% price premium and open doors to buyers who will not consider non-certified suppliers. We provide FSC certificates with every export order that specifies the requirement.

## CE Marking
Mandatory for construction products sold in the European Economic Area. Relevant standards: EN 636 (plywood), EN 300 (OSB), EN 622 (MDF), EN 312 (particleboard). CE applies to construction-grade panels; furniture-grade panels are covered by other conformity documentation.

## REACH (EU)
EU chemical safety regulation — applies to finishes, adhesives, foams and fabrics. SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) above 0.1% by weight trigger notification duties. We provide safety data sheets for coatings and adhesives on request.

## Other Standards
- ISO 9001 quality management system certification (factory-level)
- ISO 14001 environmental management
- KCMA (Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturers Association) certification available for US kitchen programs
- Australia: E1 minimum, ARI/F4-star equivalent boards for premium projects
- UK upholstered furniture: Furniture and Furnishings (Fire Safety) Regulations 1988 for any upholstered items in mixed containers

## Wood Packaging / Phytosanitary
Export wood packaging complies with ISPM 15 (heat treatment / fumigation marking). Some destination countries require phytosanitary certificates for wood products — we confirm destination requirements before shipping.

## Documentation Provided with Every Shipment
Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, plus applicable certificates: CARB/EPA TSCA compliance documents, FSC CoC, CE declarations, third-party test reports (formaldehyde, moisture content).
