# B2B Commercial Terms

## MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)
- Custom / OEM orders: flexible, project-based — from 1 kitchen set for trial orders to full-container programs
- Trial orders: low MOQ supported for new partners testing the market
- RTA stock programs: typically 1 × 40HQ container per order for best pricing
- Mixed products (kitchen + wardrobe + vanity) can share one container

## Pricing Structure
- Factory-direct FOB pricing, tiered discounts by order quantity
- Quotations itemized: carcass board + door finish + hardware brand + countertop + packing mode
- Price validity: 30 days (board and hardware material costs fluctuate)
- Target-price solutions: we propose alternative material/finish combos to hit your retail price point

## Payment Terms
- Standard T/T: 30% deposit with order, 70% balance against copy of shipping documents (custom products may require 50% deposit)
- L/C at sight: irrevocable letter of credit accepted for container orders
- D/P and other terms: negotiable for established partners
- Payment currency: USD; other currencies by agreement

## Sample Policy
- Door / material samples: provided at material cost for showroom and client presentation
- Sample lead time: 5–15 days
- Sample cost typically credited against the first bulk order
- 1:1 mockup cabinets for project sign-off: quoted per project, waived at certain order volumes

## OEM / Private Label Support
- Your brand on products, cartons and installation manuals
- Catalog and marketing material support for distributors
- Exclusive regional supply arrangements negotiable for long-term partners

## Distributor / Dealer Program
- Stepped volume pricing and priority production scheduling for repeat partners
- New product previews and exclusive SKU arrangements
- Showroom sample container programs with display fixtures
- Marketing support: photography, 3D renders, product data sheets for your local market

## Quotation Process
Send: floor plans or cabinet list (spreadsheet OK), door style / finish choice, hardware preference, destination port, packing mode preference. Response within 24–48 hours with quotation, 3D proposal and loading plan. WhatsApp / email / online inquiry all supported; showroom visits in Foshan welcome.
