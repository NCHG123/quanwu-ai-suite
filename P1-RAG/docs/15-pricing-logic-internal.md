## 1. Price Structure Overview

Prices are quoted **FOB Ningbo/Shanghai** unless the customer requests CIF/DDP. Unit basis varies by product line:

| Product line | Quoting unit | FOB price range (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen cabinets (flat-pack, melamine door) | USD per linear meter of cabinet run | $180 – $320 |
| Kitchen cabinets (lacquer / PET door) | USD per linear meter | $260 – $450 |
| Kitchen cabinets (frameless, US spec, plywood box) | USD per linear meter | $240 – $420 |
| Wardrobes (sliding door) | USD per square meter | $95 – $180 |
| Wardrobes (hinged, walk-in systems) | USD per square meter | $110 – $210 |
| Bathroom vanities | USD per piece | $120 – $380 |
| Solid wood custom (stairs, wall panels) | Project-based, per drawing | Quote after engineering review |

**Price bands are planning references only.** Final quote depends on: door finish, board spec (plywood vs MDF vs particle board), hardware brand (DTC / Blum / Hettich), glass and lighting options, and order volume.

## 2. Volume Discount Ladder

| Order value (FOB, per shipment) | Discount off list |
|---|---|
| $20,000 – $50,000 | list price |
| $50,000 – $100,000 | -2% |
| $100,000 – $200,000 | -3% |
| $200,000 – $500,000 | -4% |
| Above $500,000 | -5% (sales manager approval required beyond this) |

**Floor rule:** no discount below -8% without GM approval. This floor is confidential.

## 3. Cost Drivers (know these to defend price)

1. **Board material**: plywood box adds ~$25–40 per linear meter vs particle board, but is required for US-market humidity performance.
2. **Door finish**: lacquer costs ~1.8× melamine (labor + curing time); PET is the mid-tier sweet spot.
3. **Hardware**: Blum/Hettich adds ~$8–15 per cabinet vs DTC. Sell it as an upgrade, not a default.
4. **Packing**: flat-pack maximizes container loading (~2× assembled volume); assembled shipping adds real cost but the customer may demand it.
5. **Certifications**: CARB P2 / TSCA compliant boards add ~3–5% to material cost — non-negotiable for US orders.

## 4. Quotation Workflow

1. Customer inquiry → request: drawings / cabinet list / door style / destination port.
2. Internal costing within 24h (production dept confirms feasibility).
3. Formal quotation (PI) sent within **48 hours**, validity **30 days**.
4. Quote includes: unit prices, total FOB value, packing method, lead time (30–45 days after deposit + drawing approval), payment terms (T/T 30% deposit, 70% before shipment), and container plan.
5. Follow-up cadence: day 3, day 7, day 14. After 30 days the quote expires and requires re-costing if board or hardware prices moved.

## 5. Sample Pricing

- Catalog sample (standard cabinet box): free, customer pays courier.
- Custom sample (customer's door finish + hardware): $150–400, credited back 100% on first order above $50,000.

## 6. Common Internal Pricing Scenarios

- **"Customer says Vietnam is 20% cheaper."** Standard counter: land cost comparison is not unit cost comparison — our price includes hardware grade, CARB P2 compliance, and flat-pack loading efficiency (more cabinets per container = lower landed cost per cabinet). See sales playbook (doc 16) for full script.
- **"Customer wants CIF quote."** Add estimated freight (get live rate from forwarder) + 1% insurance. Note freight validity 7 days.
- **"Customer asks for DDP to US East Coast."** Route through our US forwarder partner; add duties (cabinets HTS 9403.40, ~0% duty but Section 301 tariffs may apply — check current rate) + last-mile. Lead time +7–12 days.
