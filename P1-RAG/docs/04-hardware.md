# Hardware Guide

Hardware quality determines how a cabinet program performs after installation. We evaluate hardware by cycle counts and load ratings, not only by brand name.

## Hinges
- Opening angles: 105°, 110° standard; 165° for corner and blind-corner cabinets
- Adjustability: six-way adjustment (up/down, left/right, in/out) on all quality European-type hinges
- Cycle life: industry minimum 80,000 open/close cycles; 100,000+ available for hotel and rental programs
- Soft-close: hydraulic damper engages in the final 15–20 degrees of closing travel
- Clip-on mounting: tool-free door removal for installation and adjustment

## Drawer Slides
- Full-extension undermount slides: 100% of drawer depth travels outside the cabinet opening
- Soft-close damper engages within 50–80 mm of the closed position
- Load ratings: 40–100 kg per pair in residential programs; heavy-duty up to 150 kg for pantry and commercial use
- Push-to-open slides available for handle-less designs

## Hardware Brands
- Imported: Blum (Austria), Hettich (Germany) — specified on request, adds cost and 5–10 days lead time
- Chinese premium: DTC, NITTO, domestic soft-close systems — 80,000+ cycle tested, standard on export programs
- We verify genuine hardware brands at inspection: brand stamps on hinge arms and slide members are checked against the purchase contract.

## Structural Fittings
- Cam lock + dowel assembly: standard for RTA (ready-to-assemble) programs, fast tool-minimal installation
- Dado and rabbet construction: mechanical interlock for heavy-duty base cabinets, superior rigidity
- Confirmat screws and minifix connectors for KD (knock-down) export construction

## Countertop Options
- Artificial quartz (engineered stone) — most popular for export kitchens
- Granite, marble — natural stone, sealed
- Sintered stone / porcelain slab — heat and scratch resistant, premium tier
- Laminate post-formed — budget tier
- Solid surface (Corian-type) — seamless repairable

## Other Functional Hardware
- Lazy Susan corner carousels, pull-out baskets, Magic Corner systems
- Sink cabinets: aluminum foil lined, under-sink pull-out organizers
- Lift-up flap systems (Aventos-type) for wall cabinets
- LED strip lighting with sensor switches for wardrobe and under-cabinet applications
- Wardrobe fittings: pull-down rails, trouser racks, jewelry drawers, shoe racks
