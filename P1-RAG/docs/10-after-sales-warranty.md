# After-Sales, Installation & Warranty

## Warranty
- Standard warranty: 2–3 years on cabinets covering mechanical defects, hardware failure under normal use, and manufacturing workmanship
- Extended / lifetime structural warranty available for partner programs
- Warranty applies to factory defects; excludes improper installation, water damage from plumbing failures, normal wear and unauthorized modifications

## Warranty Claims Process
1. Customer sends photos/videos of the issue with order number and carton label
2. Our after-sales team responds within 24 hours with diagnosis
3. Replacement parts manufactured and shipped free of charge for confirmed defects
4. Replacement hardware/panels shipped with next container (sea freight) or by express for critical small parts

## Installation Support
Every shipment includes:
- Installation manual with exploded diagrams, per cabinet type
- Panel map / labeling: every panel and carton labeled by room and cabinet position
- Assembly videos (RTA programs): step-by-step for cam-lock/dowel construction
- Hardware bag inventory list per cabinet

For distributor and contractor partners:
- Remote video call guidance for installation teams (scheduled sessions)
- Train-the-trainer sessions for dealer installation crews
- Installation engineer on-site dispatch available for premium villa/hotel projects (travel costs borne by customer)

## Spare Parts Program
- Hardware spare pack included per container: extra hinges, slides, handles, adjustment cams
- Replacement door/panel reorders: produced to the retained sample record (color, profile, construction) so replacements match the original shipment years later
- Discontinued color matching: we retain edge band, board and finish records per customer for repeat consistency

## Quality Feedback Loop
Each shipment has a retained sample and QC record. Complaints are traced back to production batch records (barcode system) for root-cause analysis. Repeat-offense issues feed back into process adjustments — this is why trial-assembly QC and moisture control checkpoints matter.

## Common After-Sales Scenarios
- Transit damage: documented via loading photos + delivery photos; claims handled with marine insurance; replacements prioritized in next production run
- Hardware adjustment issues (door alignment after assembly): remote guidance video, adjustment instructions in manual
- Color variation between shipments: minimized by retained samples and same-batch board sourcing; we flag any substrate batch change before production
