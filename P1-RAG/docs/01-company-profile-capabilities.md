# Company Profile & Manufacturing Capabilities

We are a full-house customization furniture factory based in Foshan, Guangdong, China — the core furniture manufacturing hub. We specialize in made-to-order kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, bathroom vanities, TV units, closet systems and complete whole-house casegood packages for overseas B2B clients: renovation and fit-out contractors, real estate developers, distributors and local full-house customization companies.

## Factory Overview
- Production facility: 50,000+ square meters with 4 workshops (panel cutting, edge banding, drilling, assembly and packing)
- Annual capacity: 150,000+ sets of custom cabinets
- Workforce: 250+ employees including 8 in-house designers and a dedicated export documentation team
- Export experience: serving 100+ countries across Oceania, North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Southeast Asia
- Management: intelligent ERP production management system with order tracking from cutting list to container loading

## Production Equipment
Our workshop runs a fully digital production line: automatic panel saws, CNC machining centers, automatic edge banders (PUR hot-melt available on request), multi-row drilling machines, and a barcode-driven sorting system. Every panel carries a barcode linked to the customer's cutting list, which keeps custom sizes traceable and repeat orders consistent.

## OEM / ODM Services
- OEM: we manufacture to your drawings, specifications and brand. Private labeling, custom carton printing and brand packaging are supported.
- ODM: our design team develops product lines for your market based on local aesthetics, standard dimensions and price targets.
- Design collaboration: we accept floor plans, elevation drawings, 3D models and cut lists. We return shop drawings, 3D renderings and cutting lists for your confirmation before production.

## Markets & Project Types
We serve three main B2B customer types:
1. Renovation / fit-out companies — one-off residential kitchens and whole-house packages, semi-custom, mixed containers.
2. Real estate developers — multi-unit apartment and villa programs with repeated layouts, standardized carcasses, phased deliveries.
3. Overseas cabinet companies and distributors — RTA (ready-to-assemble) stock programs, private label production, container-load stocking orders.

## Quality Control
Quality control runs at four checkpoints: incoming material inspection, post-cutting inspection, edge banding and drilling inspection, and 100% pre-packing trial assembly. Each shipment has a retained sample record (color, door profile, construction) so repeat shipments match the approved standard. Wood substrate moisture content is controlled within 8%–12% to prevent post-export warping.
